About iCapital Finance

Tailored, personalised and strategic property financing advice for homeowners, investors and businesses.

Who you will work with

Personal service, backed by over 38 lenders

iCapital Finance is a mortgage broker in Glen Waverley, Victoria, led by Wendy Lee. When you work with iCapital, Wendy looks after your loan herself, from the first conversation through to settlement, and comes back to you every year with a complimentary loan review.

Behind that personal service is the reach of a full brokerage. We are accredited with over 38 lenders, from Australia’s Big 4 banks to a wide array of smaller banks and specialist lenders, and draw on over two decades of experience in accounting, investment banking and mortgage lending. We analyse your financial situation, compare the options across that panel, and present the ones that best meet your goals while keeping interest and fees down.

Clients and referral partners say the same thing about Wendy: she is easy to reach, quick to respond, and keeps going on the deals other lenders find too complex.
