About iCapital Finance

Tailored, personalised and strategic property financing advice for homeowners, investors and businesses.

Who we are

An Australian mortgage broker that is fully invested in your goals

iCapital Finance is a mortgage broker based in Glen Waverley, Victoria. Our team brings over two decades of experience in accounting, investment banking and mortgage lending, and we use it to help you build wealth through property.

We are accredited with over 38 lenders, from Australia’s Big 4 banks to a wide array of smaller banks and specialist lenders. We analyse your financial situation, compare the options across that panel, and present the ones that best meet your goals while keeping interest and fees down.

Clients and referral partners work with Wendy Lee from the first conversation through to settlement.
